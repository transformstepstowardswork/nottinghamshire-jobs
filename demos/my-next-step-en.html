What the worker would receive

“Could we talk about this?”

    Private Stories, other opportunities and the rest of Alex's life are not automatically shared.

    Human judgement stays human. The worker may know Alex's circumstances, ask different questions, and help with things the app cannot decide.
    Another day, another reason

    The app doesn't need the whole journey.

    Someone else might start somewhere entirely different. No feature is a prerequisite for another.

    Some people use MNS often. Some only occasionally. Some receive valuable human support without using it at all.

    The promise and the boundary

    Not a process to finish.
    A presence you can return to.

    MNS does not decide who Alex is, prescribe a career, replace a worker, or claim credit for a job. Its role is smaller—and more useful for being clear.

    “Support the person, not the completion of a record.”

    About this presentation

    This is a fictional, self-contained presentation. Interactions are scripted illustrations that run entirely in your browser. No real participant data, CV, vacancy, audio, support request or application is sent, stored on a server or assessed by an AI model.

    The examples illustrate the intended My Next Step experience and design philosophy; they do not independently prove which features are live in a particular deployment. Transform presentation colours are used with permission of the supplied source design.
