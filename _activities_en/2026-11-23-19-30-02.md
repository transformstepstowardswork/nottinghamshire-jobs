AI can help you understand a job advert or organise your experience, but its answers can also be inaccurate or overconfident.

This one-hour introduction explores how to use AI as a support tool while keeping control of your applications. The session includes use of Transform's My Next Step app. No previous AI experience is needed.

## What we will explore

- Understanding job adverts and identifying their key requirements.
- Organising your real experience into relevant skills and evidence.
- Developing ideas for CVs, applications and interview practice.
- Checking and improving AI suggestions before using them.
- Keeping control of personal information and what you submit.

The aim is to support your thinking, without inventing qualifications, producing misleading applications or allowing a tool to make decisions for you.
