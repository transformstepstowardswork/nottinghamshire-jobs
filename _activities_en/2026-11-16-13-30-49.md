Are you spending time applying for jobs but not hearing back?

Perhaps you have valuable work experience from the UK or another country, along with volunteering or life experience, but you are not sure how to explain it clearly to employers here.

Or maybe you have not updated your CV for a while and are unsure what UK employers expect now.

Sometimes the problem is not a lack of skills or experience. It is that your CV is not showing employers what you could bring to the role.

## What we will cover

At this free CV & Application Masterclass, we will look at:

- what to include in a UK CV, and what you can leave out;
- how to turn work, volunteering and life experience into clear skills and strengths;
- how to read a job advert and identify what the employer is really looking for;
- how to tailor your CV to the role you want to apply for; and
- how to introduce yourself more clearly on an application form.

## A practical session

This is a practical workshop, not just a talk. You will have the chance to work through exercises and identify parts of your CV that could be improved.

Bring your CV if you have one. If there is a job you want to apply for, bring the job advert too. If you have not prepared anything yet, that is fine — you are still welcome.

## Steps Towards Work

The workshop is part of the Steps Towards Work programme. Alongside the classes, we can offer one-to-one employment support with improving your CV, preparing applications and getting ready for interviews.
