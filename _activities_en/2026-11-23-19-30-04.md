Interviews can feel unfamiliar when you are returning to work or bringing experience from another country. You do not need perfect answers: understanding the question and practising how to explain your experience is a useful starting point.

This two-hour masterclass provides opportunities to practise in a supportive group.

## What we will explore

- UK interview expectations and common formats.
- Introducing yourself and explaining your interest in a role.
- Answering competency questions with clear examples, including the STAR structure.
- Communication, body language and useful questions to ask an employer.
- Practice questions and constructive feedback.

When needed, Steps Towards Work participants can also arrange individual mock interviews linked to a real vacancy.
