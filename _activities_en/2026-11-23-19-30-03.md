A CV is more than a list of previous jobs. The challenge is to choose what matters to the employer and explain your experience clearly.

This two-hour workshop explores UK CV expectations and ways to provide stronger evidence in an application.

## What we will explore

- CV structure, including information to include or leave out.
- Transferable skills from work, volunteering and life experience.
- Reading job adverts and recognising an employer's requirements.
- Tailoring a CV or application to a particular role.
- Supporting statements and the use of relevant examples.

There will be time for a practical exercise. Bring your CV or a vacancy you are interested in if you have one; there is no need to prepare either in advance.

Steps Towards Work participants can also access ongoing individual CV and application support without waiting for another workshop.
