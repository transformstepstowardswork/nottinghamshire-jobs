You may have years of experience, qualifications and skills, yet still find UK recruitment unfamiliar. Or you may be returning to work after a break and wondering where to start.

This masterclass is led by Teresa Cullen, Transform's senior trainer, with more than 30 years of experience in UK recruitment and employment training. Discussion, exercises and reflection will help you understand employers' expectations and explore realistic ways forward.

## What we will explore

- How UK recruitment and workplace expectations may differ from your previous experience.
- What employers look for, and how to explain your skills and experience with confidence.
- Job-search channels, networking and ways to find suitable opportunities.
- Everyday workplace communication, including introductions, small talk and professional behaviour.
- Practical questions and ideas for your next steps.

You do not need to have chosen a particular job or career direction before attending.
