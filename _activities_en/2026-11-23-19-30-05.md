Working well with people involves more than technical knowledge. Communication, reliability and the way you handle everyday situations can make a difference.

You may already use these abilities at work, while volunteering or in daily life. This two-hour workshop helps you recognise, practise and talk about them.

## What we will explore

- Active listening, communication and teamwork.
- Reliability, professional boundaries and confidentiality.
- Basic safeguarding awareness and knowing when to seek support.
- Giving and receiving feedback, and working with people from different backgrounds.
- Turning everyday examples into evidence for CVs and interviews.

The session includes discussion and practical group activities. It is the fifth core module in the Steps Towards Work series.
