If you spend time in Beeston, you may have waited for a bus or tram on Middle Street. Beside that busy road is another place worth knowing: Middle Street Resource Centre, or MSRC.

It is a community centre focused on mental health. Alongside support services, its public community café offers somewhere to pause or meet a friend.

## Start with a coffee

In the original newsletter, I described a visit for lunch: hot coffee, a jacket potato with beans and cheese, and a plate of salad that pleasantly surprised me. That was my experience at the time, rather than a promise about today's menu or prices.

Volunteers help run the café, and the centre also has a garden. Check the [café information](https://msrc.org.uk/cafe/) and [centre website](https://msrc.org.uk/) before visiting.

## Ask about wellbeing support

MSRC offers wellbeing groups and one-to-one support. Participation arrangements and capacity can vary; consult its timetable or contact the centre.

**Update, 23 November 2026:** the website currently says online self-referrals are paused because of demand. If another organisation or support worker is already helping you, ask them to contact the centre. Otherwise, call or email to ask about a self-referral. Follow the [current referral guidance](https://msrc.org.uk/self-referral-2/).

The old newsletter's health-talk timetable and free-check arrangements have not been republished as a standing service. Please confirm them with the centre.

Getting to know a community can begin with a small step. Next time you pass Middle Street, you might take a look and discover another local place to learn about community life and support.
