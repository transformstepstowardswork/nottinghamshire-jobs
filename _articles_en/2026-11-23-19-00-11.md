Do you know a young person who has recently left school, is looking for work, or has not yet decided what comes next?

The King's Trust, formerly The Prince's Trust, helps young people develop confidence and skills while exploring work, learning and enterprise. The original newsletter introduced several options using information from its East Midlands team.

## Begin with confidence and practical experience

**Explore** uses group challenges, communication, teamwork and individual support to help young people recognise their abilities. The newsletter described it for 16–25-year-olds outside work, education and training.

**Team** is a 12-week personal development and employment preparation programme, combining community projects, activities and skills learning. The newsletter mentioned opportunities in Nottingham, Mansfield and Sutton-in-Ashfield for ages 16–25.

Young people interested in the NHS, care or related work can also ask about **Health & Social Care** information and training support. The information session described in the newsletter was aimed at ages 16–30.

Dates, places and eligibility change. This article retains the introduction to these resources; ask the organiser about the December 2026 and early-2027 courses mentioned in the newsletter, rather than treating an earlier announcement as confirmation of a place.

## Development Awards: help with essential costs

Official guidance describes Development Awards of £10–£500 for eligible young people aged 16–30 facing a financial barrier to learning or starting work. These are not general living-cost payments. Location, work or study hours, an identified opportunity and financial circumstances affect eligibility.

Do not buy items first and expect reimbursement. Allow time to apply and read the [official Development Awards guidance](https://www.kingstrust.org.uk/help-for-young-people/get-funding-train-learn).

## CV review: ask about current arrangements

The newsletter also introduced a free CV Review Service, with corporate volunteers commenting on English CVs, cover letters or application answers. It supplied CVReview@kingstrust.org.uk as the enquiry address. Before submitting, confirm current availability, age eligibility, file format, turnaround and privacy arrangements. The old response times are not a guarantee.

## You can ask before choosing a programme

The newsletter supplied kingstrusteastmidlands@kingstrust.org.uk for the East Midlands team. Introduce your age, area, work or study situation and the help you are seeking. Obtain a young person's consent before making a referral on their behalf.

You can also start with [The King's Trust website](https://www.kingstrust.org.uk/).

Not every programme will suit everyone. But trying something new and meeting people can be the beginning of finding a direction.
