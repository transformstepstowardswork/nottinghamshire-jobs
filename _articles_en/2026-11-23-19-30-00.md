Have you wanted to join our employment workshops but found it difficult to fit them around weekday work, family responsibilities or other commitments?

Transform Training is offering a new Steps Towards Work core programme across two Saturdays: 5 and 12 December 2026.

Our workshops have usually taken place on weekdays. This weekend intake gives people who cannot easily attend during the week another opportunity to take part.

The five core modules total 12 hours, covering UK recruitment, CVs, interviews, AI-assisted job seeking and working with people. Alongside learning practical techniques, you will have opportunities to reflect on your experience and build confidence in finding suitable work.

## Saturday 5 December

### 10:00–15:00 | How to Get a Job in the UK Masterclass

Explore employers' expectations, job-search routes, workplace culture and communication, and learn to explain the value of your work and life experience.

[Find out more and register](https://www.eventbrite.co.uk/e/how-to-get-a-job-in-the-uk-masterclass-steps-towards-work-tickets-2004759348177)

### 15:00–16:00 | AI-Assisted Job Seeking

Learn how AI can help you analyse job adverts, organise experience and prepare applications, while checking and editing its suggestions.

[Find out more and register](https://www.eventbrite.co.uk/e/ai-assisted-job-seeking-steps-towards-work-tickets-2005270863132)

## Saturday 12 December

### 10:00–12:00 | CV & Application Masterclass

Explore UK-style CVs, recognise transferable skills and adapt your CV, application or supporting statement to a job advert.

[Find out more and register](https://www.eventbrite.co.uk/e/cv-application-masterclass-steps-towards-work-tickets-2005270524118)

### 12:00–14:00 | Interview Skills Masterclass

Learn about common UK interview approaches, practise introducing yourself and answering questions about your abilities and experience, and build confidence through feedback.

[Find out more and register](https://www.eventbrite.co.uk/e/interview-skills-masterclass-steps-towards-work-tickets-2005269529142)

### 14:00–16:00 | Working with People: Volunteer & Workplace Skills

Use volunteering and workplace situations to explore communication, teamwork, listening, responsibility and professional boundaries, then apply those experiences to CVs and interviews.

[Find out more and register](https://www.eventbrite.co.uk/e/working-with-people-volunteer-workplace-skills-steps-towards-work-tickets-2005269825027)

## Five core modules across two Saturdays

Participants who complete all five modules and 12 hours of training, and meet the course requirements, can receive a course certificate. Core modules can also be completed across different programme cycles, without a fixed order.

All sessions take place at Transform City Hub, Room 3, Carlton Road Business Centre, 27 Carlton Road, Nottingham NG3 2DG. The listings specify ages 18+. Sessions are free for eligible participants, with eligibility and places confirmed by Transform Training. Booking a workshop does not itself complete programme enrolment.

If you are looking for work in the UK, returning to work or hoping to improve your job-search skills, follow the individual links to learn more and register. Please share the programme with friends who find weekday training difficult to attend.

For questions or support needs, contact Anthony Kan on 07383 549913 or [anthony.k@transformtraining.org.uk](mailto:anthony.k@transformtraining.org.uk).
