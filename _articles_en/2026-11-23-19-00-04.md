A long job description can leave you unsure where to begin. AI can help organise the information and give you a partner for interview practice.

## Ask AI to break down the advert

Paste the public text of the advert and try this prompt:

> Please organise this job advert into essential requirements, desirable criteria, main duties, relevant skills and important terms. Use evidence from the advert, separate explicit requirements from your assumptions, and flag anything I should check with the employer.

Compare the result with your experience, then choose examples that show the relevant abilities. Use appropriate workplace language in your CV and cover letter, without forcing in keywords or claiming qualifications you do not hold. Employers use different screening methods, and AI cannot know a recruiter's unwritten thoughts.

## Use AI as an interview practice partner

If you know the STAR method but struggle to make it sound natural, try:

> Act as a UK hiring manager and ask one question at a time about this role. After each answer, comment on clarity and evidence, then help me improve it using Situation, Task, Action and Result. Use only my real experience; do not invent actions, results or figures.

Practise saying your answers aloud, rather than only reading a polished paragraph. Keep your own voice and make sure you can explain every sentence in an interview.

## Check what comes back

Treat the analysis and feedback as practice material, rather than an employer's actual assessment. Check it against the original advert. Avoid entering private contact details, identity numbers or other people's personal information, and understand the privacy settings of the service you choose.
