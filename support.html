Support from Transform Training

Support to help you move forward

Everyone’s situation is different. Our support is designed to work with you over time, understand what you need, and help you make realistic progress towards your next step.

← Back to Transform Jobs

We currently offer two support pathways.

Young people

If you are aged 18–24

You can receive ongoing one-to-one support alongside group sessions designed around the needs of young people.

Sessions can help you build confidence, improve wellbeing and communication, recognise your strengths, set goals and plan your next steps.

The aim is to help you move towards employment, education or training.

Adults

If you are aged 25 or over

You can receive ongoing one-to-one employability support alongside practical group training.

Support can include:

  • CVs and job applications
  • interview preparation
  • job searching
  • workplace skills
  • confidence building
  • building UK work experience

The focus is on helping you strengthen your job search and make realistic progress towards employment.

+

Extra support when you need it

For both pathways, we can also provide individual help when a particular opportunity comes up.

This can include one-to-one CV or application coaching and a practice interview to help you prepare for a real interview.

How support begins

Tell us what you need through our enquiry form. We can discuss which support might be right for you. Sending an enquiry does not enrol you in a programme.

For Steps Towards Work, staff will explain the funded programme face to face, check eligibility and complete the required signed application before registration.
